## Hi, I'm Mashroor Nitol 👋

**Computational Material Scientist at Discovery2scale** · Santa Fe, NM  
Formerly Technical Staff Member at **Los Alamos National Laboratory** 

I build **machine-learning interatomic potentials** and the **HPC/GPU workflows** around them — connecting DFT (VASP) and atomistic simulation (LAMMPS) with AI surrogates such as **MACE**, **moment tensor potentials**, and **neural-network potentials** to accelerate materials discovery.

### Research focus
- ML interatomic potentials — MACE / MACE-OFF fine-tuning, MTP, RANN, active learning, uncertainty quantification
- Atomistic simulation — LAMMPS (ML-IAP, embedded Python), VASP, phase stability, deformation and shock response
- AI for materials discovery — crystal-structure prediction, GNNs, domain language models, LLM literature extraction
- HPC & GPU — Slurm, MPI, multi-node / multi-GPU training on V100, A100, and H100

### Selected projects
- **CATENA** — polymer crystal-structure prediction: helix enumeration, periodic packing, MACE pre-screening, and density-aware ranking → five candidate CIFs per polymer for DFT validation
- **AlloyBERT** — DeBERTa-v3 language model pretrained from scratch on a 70k+ paper alloy corpus
- **RANN in LAMMPS** — co-developed the rapid artificial neural network potential and `pair_style rann`
- **ML potentials for alloys** — Ti, Sn, Pu, Ti–Al–V, Ag–Cu, Ti–V, and the VNbTaTiZr refractory high-entropy alloy
